## JETHRO TULL — MISPRESS RESEARCH FINDING

**Row 38 — Jethro Tull — Minstrel in the Gallery (CHR 1082)**

The "reverse side of record upside down" is **NOT a mispress or defect.** The upside-down back cover is an intentional design feature on original 1975 Chrysalis pressings of this title. Later pressings corrected it — so having the upside-down back actually authenticates this as an early pressing.

Do not flag it as damaged or unusual in the listing. The Comments text above notes it accurately for buyers who might be confused by it.

**Revised price: $24** (early pressing character, slight premium over generic used copies at $15-20).
